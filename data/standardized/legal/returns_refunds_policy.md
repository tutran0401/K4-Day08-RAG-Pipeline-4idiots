E-COMMERCE RETURNS AND REFUNDS POLICY - EDUCATIONAL SNAPSHOT
Source reference: https://help.shopee.vn/portal/4/article/77251
Customer role: buyer
Last reviewed for this lab dataset: 2026-08-04

This document is a structured educational snapshot of public customer-support guidance. The live help-center article remains the authoritative source and may change.

RETURN AND REFUND ELIGIBILITY
The buyer may submit a return/refund request within 15 days after the order is marked delivered successfully. For fresh or frozen food, the stated request window is 24 hours after successful delivery. A request may apply when an item was not received, arrived incomplete, was damaged, was materially different from its description, was counterfeit, or otherwise met a published eligibility condition. The buyer should not select “order received” before checking the parcel where the interface warns that doing so affects available remedies.

EVIDENCE
The buyer should preserve the parcel, shipping label, product, accessories and gifts while a case is reviewed. Clear photographs or an unedited video should show the entire package, the shipping label, the condition of the product, quantity, model, and the specific defect or discrepancy. Payment records or chat records may be requested when relevant. Evidence must concern the disputed order and should not expose unrelated personal information.

PROCESS
Open the order in the application, choose Return/Refund, select the truthful reason, describe the issue and upload the requested evidence. Follow the displayed return method and deadline. The platform or seller may review the evidence. Refund timing depends on the original payment channel and the result of review. Do not send a parcel outside the instructed workflow because tracking and buyer protection may be unavailable.

This snapshot does not create new rights and is not legal advice. Users must confirm deadlines and conditions in the live policy shown for their order.
