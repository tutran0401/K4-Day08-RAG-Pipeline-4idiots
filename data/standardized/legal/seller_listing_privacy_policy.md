SELLER LISTING AND PRIVACY RULES - EDUCATIONAL SNAPSHOT
Source references: public seller education and privacy/help-center pages of the selected marketplace
Customer role: both
Last reviewed for this lab dataset: 2026-08-04

This classroom document consolidates public principles used in the RAG benchmark. The marketplace’s live terms, prohibited-items list and privacy notice are authoritative.

SELLER LISTING RESPONSIBILITIES
Sellers must provide accurate titles, descriptions, photographs, variations, condition, origin, price and stock information. Listings must be placed in the correct category and must not manipulate search results, impersonate another party or make unsupported claims. A seller must have the right to sell the item and must comply with applicable law, intellectual-property rights and platform rules.

PROHIBITED CONTENT AND PRODUCTS
Sellers must not list counterfeit or imitation goods, illegal products, stolen goods, products that infringe copyright, trademark or other intellectual-property rights, or products in the marketplace’s prohibited and restricted items list. Examples can include weapons, controlled substances, unsafe products, personal data, fraudulent documents and other regulated products unless an explicit lawful exception and platform approval applies. Attempts to evade moderation through misspelling, hidden images, misleading categories or off-platform contact details are also prohibited.

ENFORCEMENT
The platform may remove a listing, restrict visibility, cancel affected transactions, request authenticity or authorization documents, limit account functions or suspend an account. Sellers should use the appeal channel and submit genuine evidence. Repeated or serious violations may lead to stronger measures.

PRIVACY PRINCIPLES
The service may process account identifiers, contact and delivery details, transaction records, device or log data, customer-support communications and information required for fraud prevention or legal compliance. Data should be used for stated purposes such as providing the service, processing orders and payments, security, support, personalization where permitted, and compliance. Users can review the live privacy notice for retention, sharing, security and applicable access or correction rights. Passwords and OTP codes must never be posted in a listing or support message.

This educational snapshot is not legal advice and must not replace the current official rules.
